Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

Do my photos get uploaded to any server or cloud? +

Never. Snapferry has zero servers, zero accounts, and zero cloud storage. Photos and videos stream directly from device memory to device memory through an encrypted WebRTC data channel over your local network.

Do both devices need to be on the same Wi-Fi? +

Yes. Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi network, or one device connected to the other's personal hotspot. If your router has "Client Isolation" turned on (common in hotels or coffee shops), personal hotspot is the easiest fix.

How does saving photos work on iPhone Safari? +

Because iOS Safari security prevents websites from writing directly into the Apple Photos camera roll, Snapferry triggers the native iOS Share Sheet (navigator.share) so you can tap "Save Image" or "Save Video" with one touch. You can also download as a clean ZIP.

Is there a file size limit? +

No artificial limit. Our custom transfer engine slices files into 16 KB chunks and applies backpressure to pause transmission when the buffer fills up, allowing large multi-gigabyte 4K videos or batches of 50+ photos without crashing browser memory.

Can I use this between two browser tabs on the same computer? +

Yes! Every step includes a "Copy code / Paste code" button so laptops without webcams or two tabs on the same machine can pair seamlessly without camera access.

Can't connect? Quick checklist

📶
Same Wi-Fi network: Make sure both devices are connected to the exact same Wi-Fi router or access point (not one on 5GHz guest and one on private).
🛡️
Turn off VPNs: VPN apps (iCloud Private Relay, NordVPN, Tailscale, etc.) route your traffic away from the local subnet. Turn them off temporarily.
🚫
Client Isolation: Many public Wi-Fi networks (hotels, cafes, schools) intentionally block devices from seeing each other.
⚡
Best solution: Personal Hotspot! Turn on Personal Hotspot on one phone and connect the other phone or laptop to it. This always works 100% of the time, completely offline!
